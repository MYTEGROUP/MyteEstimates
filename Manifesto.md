# Statement of Intent - The Seed

I am open-sourcing the original seed I wrote in Mexico in December 2023. That seed led me to where I am now. It is the doorway to MYTE: a way to translate chaotic intent into structured, executable artifacts. It was built over two intense weeks and finished on New Year's Eve. It confirmed what I needed to know: digital intelligence, paired with deep domain experience, can solve the hardest problem in business and society - understanding each other well enough to converge on outcomes that work.

This repository is not the full platform. It is the seed. Some journeys must be lived to be understood. The rest of the platform remains private by choice, not because I want to lock anyone out, but because the responsibility and the learning cannot be skipped. This seed exists so builders can study it, challenge it, and adapt it to their own domains.

The core idea is simple: translate intent into structure. If we can turn raw needs into clear artifacts, we can execute faster, estimate better, and reduce the communication loss that destroys projects. That translation layer is the real breakthrough.

My choice at the time was OpenAI. In December 2023, it was the only platform available to me that could support this exploration. I will always acknowledge that. But I also keep my eyes open. Technology is a tool. It will never be as good as the human mind. We build for humans, by humans, and the systems we create must reflect that.

I am a builder. I can build for fourteen hours straight because this is what I love to do. I found my ikigai at the intersection of what I know, what I love, what I can be paid for, and what the world needs. I am not threatened by AI; I am amplified by it. And I hope these systems keep getting better, because there is still so much to build, understand, and repair.

This seed is free and open. Do what you want with it. Run it, test it, inspect the artifacts, verify the logic. Fork it and grow it into your own system. If you build on it, attribution is appreciated.

I care about values and boundaries. Transparency where it matters. Privacy where it must be protected. Full observability in work, contribution, and value exchange - without power imbalances. Guardrails instead of control. Throughput instead of bureaucracy. A new definition of compliance, one we will keep experimenting with.

I have spent my life taking complex organizations and turning them into structured systems. This seed is a public trace of how I think. It is only one piece of the puzzle. Other builders are solving other pieces. My goal is to create a safe place for people to work and to orchestrate systems where everyone feels respected and aligned.

If you want to build your own system, start here. If you want to collaborate, join us at Myte Group Inc. The world needs more builders. There is so much to build, and I plan to keep building it with all of you.
